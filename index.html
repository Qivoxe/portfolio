01 · About

Proofs in the background,
models in the foreground.

Hey, I'm Shivam — a BSc Mathematics (Hons) student at the University of Delhi working at the boundary where formal mathematics meets learned systems.

I came to machine learning through proof. Probability and linear algebra stopped being coursework and became tools for reasoning about why models converge, when they generalize, and where they fail. That obsession with explainable behavior — not just benchmark scores — defines how I work.

My backend engineering exists for one reason: to serve mathematics at scale. FastAPI inference APIs, containerized models, CI/CD and observability — infrastructure as rigorous as the math it hosts. Friends don't let friends deploy models they can't explain.
